Privacy Policy

Sandali — an internal tool for scheduling videos to YouTube.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Overview

Sandali ("the app") is a private, internal-use application operated by John Lloyd, the owner of this website. It is not offered to the public and has no public sign-up, user accounts, or third-party users. Its only purpose is to schedule and upload videos that the operator has already produced to the operator's own YouTube channel.

Google user data we access

Sandali connects to Google using OAuth 2.0 with the installed-app flow and requests a single scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl. Through the YouTube Data API v3, the app accesses the following, and only for the operator's own channel:

  • video files the operator selects for upload;
  • video metadata: title, description, tags, category, and thumbnail;
  • the scheduled publish time and privacy status; and
  • the IDs and processing status of the videos the app uploaded.

The app does not access your name, email address, contacts, Google Drive files, calendar, or any other Google service. It has no access to other users' channels or data.

How we use Google user data

Google user data is used only to:

  • upload a video the operator selected, as private, with a scheduled publish time;
  • read back the upload's status so the local queue can report success or failure; and
  • update or delete the videos that the app itself uploaded.

There is no profiling, advertising, analytics, or model training, and no use for any purpose unrelated to these functions.

Storage and security

  • The OAuth client ID, client secret, and refresh token are stored in a local .env file on the operator's machine. That file is excluded from version control.
  • Access tokens are held in memory only for the duration of a request.
  • No Google user data is sent to or stored on any third-party server. Video files go directly from the operator's machine to Google's upload endpoint.
  • The app keeps no database of viewers and no unrelated account data.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share Google user data with third parties. We do not use it for advertising, for determining creditworthiness or lending eligibility, or for any purpose unrelated to the app's single function. Only the operator can access the app and its local credentials.

Retention and deletion

  • The refresh token is retained until the operator revokes access or deletes the local .env file.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all future API calls immediately.
  • Uploaded videos can be deleted at any time in YouTube Studio or through the app.

Cookies and analytics

This website and the app do not set cookies and do not use analytics or tracking. These pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive standard request information such as your IP address as described in Google's privacy policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Sandali's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than its operator.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The date at the top of this page reflects the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@johnlloyd.dev. See also the Sandali Terms of Service.
